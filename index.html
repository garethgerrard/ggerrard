About

Gareth Gerrard is a Birmingham-based film director. His films explore self and shadow, memory, and the quieter shades of the human condition.

A background in photography, video, and music, together with a Master of Arts in Art and Design, informs a visual language that is precise, withheld, and shaped as much by absence as by image.

He is developing new short films and feature-length projects, produced under his own banner, Altar Films.
